# Deepikaz Art — Deepika Arivazhagan's website

Production website for Deepika Arivazhagan (live wedding painting & custom art, @deepikaz.art):
gallery of her paintings, brochure price guide with finish explainer, Paint & Play corner,
and an enquiry form that opens the visitor's details in Deepika's WhatsApp (wa.me handoff).

Live: https://deepikaz-art.vercel.app/ · Backup: https://lalitha-kesaraju.github.io/deepikaz-art-demo/

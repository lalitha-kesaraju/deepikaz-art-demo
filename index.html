How live painting works

From first enquiry to a finished painting on your wall — this is the journey on your wedding day.

1 Enquire & block your date

Send the enquiry with your date and city. A Rs 5,000 advance blocks Deepika's calendar for your day.

2 She arrives early

Deepika reaches the venue 2–3 hours before the event, sets up her easel and paints the scene and decor first.

3 Painted live, 5–7 hours

As your celebration unfolds, the couple and the moment come alive on canvas — your guests watch it happen.

4 Handover

Studio finishing touches are added where needed, and the painting is handed over. Balance is paid on handover.

Paint & Play — try painting yourself

While you're here, visit our little studio corner: colour a wedding couple or a peacock, or grab a brush and paint anything you like on a blank canvas. Download your artwork when you're done.

Which finish should you pick?

The question almost every couple asks Deepika — here it is in her own words, from her brochure.

Why Classic?

Minimal background work + good character work. Done and handed over on the spot.

Why Signature?

Better background work + detailed character work. Done and handed over on the spot.

Why Studio Touch?

Detailed background work + detailed character work. Deepika takes the canvas to her studio for better detailing.

Why Heirloom?

Studio Touch + high detailing with rework. The flowers, decor and characters are reworked inch by inch in the studio from an HD pic by your photographer — even the jewels are re-done in minute work. A masterpiece of the moment.

How live painting works

From first enquiry to a finished painting on your wall — this is the journey on your wedding day.

1 Enquire & block your date

Send the enquiry with your date and city. An advance blocks Deepika's calendar for your day (amount in the brochure).

2 She arrives early

Deepika reaches the venue 2–3 hours before the event, sets up her easel and paints the scene and decor first.

3 Painted live, 5–7 hours

As your celebration unfolds, the couple and the moment come alive on canvas — your guests watch it happen.

4 Handover

Studio finishing touches are added where needed, and the painting is handed over. Balance is paid on handover.

Which finish should you pick?

The question almost every couple asks Deepika — here it is in her own words, from her brochure.

Why Classic?

Minimal background work + good character work. Done and handed over on the spot.

Why Signature?

Better background work + detailed character work. Done and handed over on the spot.

Why Studio Refined Classic?

Detailed background work + detailed character work. Deepika takes the canvas to her studio for better detailing.

Why Studio Refined Signature?

Studio refined work + high detailing with rework. The flowers, decor and characters are reworked inch by inch in the studio from an HD pic by your photographer — even the jewels are re-done in minute work. A masterpiece of the moment.

Why Hybrid?

Deepika's hybrid painting format, offered in 20×24, 24×30 and 24×36. Mention Hybrid in your enquiry and she will walk you through how it works for your event.

How booking works: An advance blocks your date (amount in the brochure), and the balance is paid on handover of the finished painting. On the day, Deepika arrives 2–3 hours early and paints live for 5–7 hours as your event unfolds. Travel to the venue is charged extra, at actuals. For custom paintings from a photo and workshop access, send an enquiry and she will confirm your exact quote.

What's included

1 Live painting

Your ceremony / key moment painted on canvas at the venue, in front of your guests.

2 Studio finish

Higher finishes (Studio Refined and above) include extra studio detailing after the event.

3 Memories kept

A physical heirloom of the day — not just photos and video, but a painting made inside the moment.

4 Worldwide

60+ events painted, across India and worldwide. Share your city in the enquiry.
